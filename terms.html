Cliping – Terms of Service

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Cliping is a personal tool that runs on the owner's own computer. It turns the owner's long videos and live streams into short clips and, when the owner chooses, posts them to the owner's own TikTok and YouTube accounts.

Use

  • Cliping is used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public.
  • The owner only processes and posts videos they have the right to use.
  • Nothing is posted without the owner choosing to post it.

Posting uses TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API. Use of TikTok is also subject to TikTok's own Terms of Service.
