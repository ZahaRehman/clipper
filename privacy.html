Cliping – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Cliping is a personal tool that runs only on its owner's computer. It is not a public service and has no other users.

What Cliping stores

  • The videos the owner chooses to process, and the clips made from them.
  • From TikTok: the owner's display name and the access tokens TikTok returns after the owner logs in (scopes user.info.basic and video.publish).

All of this stays on the owner's computer. It is not sold, shared, or sent anywhere except to TikTok when the owner posts a clip.

How TikTok data is used

The access token is used only to show which account is connected and to upload clips the owner chooses to post.

Deleting data

Disconnecting TikTok in Cliping deletes the stored tokens. Access can also be removed at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The date above shows the latest version.
